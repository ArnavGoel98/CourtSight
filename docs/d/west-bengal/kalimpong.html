How long do court cases take in kalimpong?

The eCourts records cannot answer this. They hold 2,771 cases filed here in 2010–2017 but only 2,308 decisions recorded in those years, against 985 in 2018 alone. Decisions before 2018 were evidently not entered, so almost every case would look stuck.

Published naively, kalimpong would rank among the slowest courts in India. An earlier version of this project did exactly that. A check now requires a district's records to show decisions as well as filings before its figures are used. kalimpong never passes, so its figures are withheld rather than shown wrong.

For a sense of the region, see the other districts in West Bengal; note that their figures may not apply here.
