How long do court cases take in Gangtok?

Half of the civil suit cases filed here in 2010–2018 were decided within 2 months (all of India: 2.5 years). 5% were still pending three years after filing (India: 44%), which ranks Gangtok 1 of 577 (1 = fewest pending; 90% range 1–2): clearly among the fastest fifth.

Judges here stayed a median of 18 months in one courtroom (all districts: 12), and about 3 courtrooms were active in an average month of 2014–2017. Across districts, longer postings go with faster civil cases, but this is a correlation, not a cause (details).

Read this with care

  • Records here are complete only from 2013. Cases filed earlier are counted from then, a correction for the uneven arrival of eCourts.
  • Official filing figures for this state are too small or erratic to check how complete the records are.
  • "Decided" means any final disposal: judgment, dismissal, withdrawal, settlement or transfer. A slow district is not necessarily a badly run one: case mix, vacancies and local practice differ.
