How long do court cases take in East Garo Hills?

The case records cannot answer this. They hold 1,205 cases filed here in 2010–2017 but only 1,018 decisions recorded in those years, against 261 in 2018 alone. Decisions before 2018 were evidently not entered, so almost every case would look stuck.

Published naively, East Garo Hills would rank among the slowest courts in India. An earlier version of this project did exactly that. A check now requires a district's records to show decisions as well as filings before its figures are used. East Garo Hills never passes, so its figures are withheld rather than shown wrong.

For a sense of the region, see the other districts in Meghalaya; note that their figures may not apply here.
