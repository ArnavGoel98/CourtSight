Read this with care

  • Odisha's eCourts records hold about 56% of the cases the official figures say were filed in 2015–18. Figures here describe the courts eCourts recorded.
  • In Orissa, 29% of cases recorded as pending had no hearing listed in the year before the data was collected, so some may in fact have been decided. Delays here may be overstated.
