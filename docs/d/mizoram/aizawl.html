How long do court cases take in Aizawl?

Half of the civil suit cases filed here in 2010–2018 were decided within 3.6 years (all of India: 2.4 years). 55% were still pending three years after filing (India: 43%), which ranks Aizawl 441 of 582 (1 = fewest pending; 90% range 396–472).

Judges here stayed a median of 16 months in one courtroom (all districts: 12), and about 4 courtrooms were active in an average month of 2014–2017. Across districts, longer postings go with faster civil cases, but this is a correlation, not a cause (details).

Of the cases pending here when the data was collected, 5% were next listed for summons, notices or appearance (India: 37%), and the next hearing was set a typical 43 days after the last one (India: 56). Hearing records in this state look stale, so these gaps may reflect record-keeping. More

Read this with care

  • Records here are complete only from 2016. Cases filed earlier are counted from then, a correction for the uneven arrival of eCourts.
  • This district has few cases of this type, so its rank is uncertain (anywhere from 396 to 472).
  • Official filing figures for this state are too small or erratic to check how complete the records are.
  • In Mizoram, 22% of cases recorded as pending had no hearing listed in the year before the data was collected, so some may in fact have been decided. Delays here may be overstated.
  • "Decided" means any final disposal: judgment, dismissal, withdrawal, settlement or transfer. A slow district is not necessarily a badly run one: case mix, vacancies and local practice differ.
