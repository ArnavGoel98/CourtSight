How long do court cases take in Karbi Anglong?

The case records cannot answer this. They hold 2,857 cases filed here in 2010–2017 but only 30 decisions recorded in those years, against 2,629 in 2018 alone. Decisions before 2018 were evidently not entered, so almost every case would look stuck.

Published naively, Karbi Anglong would rank among the slowest courts in India. An earlier version of this project did exactly that. A check now requires a district's records to show decisions as well as filings before its figures are used. Karbi Anglong never passes, so its figures are withheld rather than shown wrong.

For a sense of the region, see the other districts in Assam; note that their figures may not apply here.
