How long do court cases take in KHUNTI?

The eCourts records cannot answer this. They hold 1,844 cases filed here in 2010–2017 but only 40 decisions recorded in those years, against 2,035 in 2018 alone. Decisions before 2018 were evidently not entered, so almost every case would look stuck.

Published naively, KHUNTI would rank among the slowest courts in India. An earlier version of this project did exactly that. A check now requires a district's records to show decisions as well as filings before its figures are used. KHUNTI never passes, so its figures are withheld rather than shown wrong.

For a sense of the region, see the other districts in Jharkhand; note that their figures may not apply here.
