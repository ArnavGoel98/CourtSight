How long do court cases take in Karauli?

Half of the civil suit cases filed here in 2010–2018 were decided within 2.1 years (all of India: 2.4 years). 38% were still pending three years after filing (India: 43%), which ranks Karauli 230 of 582 (1 = fewest pending; 90% range 210–248).

Judges here stayed a median of 12 months in one courtroom (all districts: 12), and about 15 courtrooms were active in an average month of 2014–2017. Across districts, longer postings go with faster civil cases, but this is a correlation, not a cause (details).

Read this with care

  • Records here are complete only from 2014. Cases filed earlier are counted from then, a correction for the uneven arrival of eCourts.
  • Rajasthan's eCourts records hold about 48% of the cases the official figures say were filed in 2015–18. Figures here describe the courts eCourts recorded.
  • "Decided" means any final disposal: judgment, dismissal, withdrawal, settlement or transfer. A slow district is not necessarily a badly run one: case mix, vacancies and local practice differ.
