How long do court cases take in Daman?

Half of the civil suit cases filed here in 2010–2018 were decided within 15 months (all of India: 2.5 years). 25% were still pending three years after filing (India: 44%), which ranks Daman 78 of 577 (1 = fewest pending; 90% range 45–121).

Read this with care

  • Records here are complete only from 2011. Cases filed earlier are counted from then, a correction for the uneven arrival of eCourts.
  • This district has few cases of this type, so its rank is uncertain (anywhere from 45 to 121).
  • Official filing figures for this state are too small or erratic to check how complete the records are.
  • "Decided" means any final disposal: judgment, dismissal, withdrawal, settlement or transfer. A slow district is not necessarily a badly run one: case mix, vacancies and local practice differ.
