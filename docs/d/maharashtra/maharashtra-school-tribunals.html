How long do court cases take in Maharashtra-School Tribunals?

Half of the other civil application cases filed here in 2010–2018 were decided within 23 months (all of India: 11 months). 36% were still pending three years after filing (India: 24%), which ranks Maharashtra-School Tribunals 380 of 575 (1 = fewest pending; 90% range 353–401).

Judges here stayed a median of 28 months in one courtroom (all districts: 12), and about 5 courtrooms were active in an average month of 2014–2017. Across districts, longer postings go with faster civil cases, but this is a correlation, not a cause (details).

Read this with care

  • Records here are complete only from 2014. Cases filed earlier are counted from then, a correction for the uneven arrival of eCourts.
  • This is a special court or court complex, not a whole district, so it is not comparable with district courts.
  • For Maharashtra, the case records used here hold about 57% of the cases the official figures say were filed in 2015–18. Figures here describe the courts these records cover.
  • "Decided" means any final disposal: judgment, dismissal, withdrawal, settlement or transfer. A slow district is not necessarily a badly run one: case mix, vacancies and local practice differ.
