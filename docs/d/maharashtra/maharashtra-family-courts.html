How long do court cases take in Maharashtra-Family Courts?

Half of the other civil application cases filed here in 2010–2018 were decided within 12 months (all of India: 11 months). 12% were still pending three years after filing (India: 23%), which ranks Maharashtra-Family Courts 112 of 590 (1 = fewest pending; 90% range 108–115): clearly among the fastest fifth.

Judges here stayed a median of 15 months in one courtroom (all districts: 12), and about 12 courtrooms were active in an average month of 2014–2017. Across districts, longer postings go with faster civil cases, but this is a correlation, not a cause (details).

Read this with care

  • This is a special court or court complex, not a whole district, so it is not comparable with district courts.
  • Maharashtra's eCourts records hold about 57% of the cases the official figures say were filed in 2015–18. Figures here describe the courts eCourts recorded.
  • "Decided" means any final disposal: judgment, dismissal, withdrawal, settlement or transfer. A slow district is not necessarily a badly run one: case mix, vacancies and local practice differ.
