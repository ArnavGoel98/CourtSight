How long do court cases take in Mumbai Small Causes Court?

Half of the other / unclassified cases filed here in 2010–2018 were decided within 2.8 years (all of India: 15 months). 49% were still pending three years after filing (India: 27%), which ranks Mumbai Small Causes Court 490 of 565 (1 = fewest pending; 90% range 485–493): clearly among the slowest fifth.

Judges here stayed a median of 12 months in one courtroom (all districts: 12), and about 3 courtrooms were active in an average month of 2014–2017. Across districts, longer postings go with faster civil cases, but this is a correlation, not a cause (details).

Read this with care

  • This is a special court or court complex, not a whole district, so it is not comparable with district courts.
  • Maharashtra's eCourts records hold about 57% of the cases the official figures say were filed in 2015–18. Figures here describe the courts eCourts recorded.
  • "Decided" means any final disposal: judgment, dismissal, withdrawal, settlement or transfer. A slow district is not necessarily a badly run one: case mix, vacancies and local practice differ.
