How long do court cases take in Mumbai Small Causes Court?

Half of the other / unclassified cases filed here in 2010–2018 were decided within 2.8 years (all of India: 15 months). 49% were still pending three years after filing (India: 28%), which ranks Mumbai Small Causes Court 483 of 558 (1 = fewest pending; 90% range 479–486): clearly among the slowest fifth.

Judges here stayed a median of 12 months in one courtroom (all districts: 12), and about 3 courtrooms were active in an average month of 2014–2017. Across districts, longer postings go with faster civil cases, but this is a correlation, not a cause (details).

Of the cases pending here when the data was collected, 25% were next listed for summons, notices or appearance (India: 37%), and the next hearing was set a typical 37 days after the last one (India: 56). Across districts, longer gaps go with slower courts, especially for criminal cases; that is a correlation, not a cause. More

Read this with care

  • This is a special court or court complex, not a whole district, so it is not comparable with district courts.
  • For Maharashtra, the case records used here hold about 57% of the cases the official figures say were filed in 2015–18. Figures here describe the courts these records cover.
  • "Decided" means any final disposal: judgment, dismissal, withdrawal, settlement or transfer. A slow district is not necessarily a badly run one: case mix, vacancies and local practice differ.
