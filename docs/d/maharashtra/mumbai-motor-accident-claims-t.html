How long do court cases take in Mumbai Motor Accident Claims T?

Half of the motor accident claim cases filed here in 2010–2018 were decided within 3.4 years (all of India: 19 months). 54% were still pending three years after filing (India: 26%), which ranks Mumbai Motor Accident Claims T 459 of 509 (1 = fewest pending; 90% range 453–461): clearly among the slowest fifth.

Read this with care

  • Records here are complete only from 2011. Cases filed earlier are counted from then, a correction for the uneven arrival of eCourts.
  • This is a special court or court complex, not a whole district, so it is not comparable with district courts.
  • Maharashtra's eCourts records hold about 57% of the cases the official figures say were filed in 2015–18. Figures here describe the courts eCourts recorded.
  • "Decided" means any final disposal: judgment, dismissal, withdrawal, settlement or transfer. A slow district is not necessarily a badly run one: case mix, vacancies and local practice differ.
